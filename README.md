## 🛠️ Essential Campus & Career Tools

Before you write a single line of code, make sure you know how to navigate GSU's systems and jumpstart your career profile.

*   **[iport](https://iport.gsu.edu/)**: This is your go-to portal for academic data, course schedules, and registration information. Get comfortable using this early on.
*   **[Rate My Professor](https://www.ratemyprofessors.com/)**: A lifesaver for scheduling. Always check a professor's reviews before registering. A great professor can make a difficult CS concept incredibly easy to understand.
*   **[Pathways Dashboard](https://pathways-six.vercel.app/dashboard)**: Use this fantastic resource to track your academic and career pathways effectively.
*   **[Handshake](https://gsu.joinhandshake.com/)**: GSU's main platform for finding jobs and internships. Set up your profile during your first week! Recruiters from Atlanta tech companies actively look for GSU students here.

## 💻 Leveling Up Your CS Skills

Your classes will teach you theory, but getting a job requires practical skills and interview prep. Start utilizing these resources as soon as you can.

*   **[CodePath](https://www.codepath.org/)**: An absolute game-changer. They offer free, high-quality, industry-backed courses in CyberSecurity, iOS/Android Development, and Technical Interview Prep. Excellent for building your resume early.
*   **[NeetCode 150](https://neetcode.io/)**: When it's time to start preparing for technical interviews (Data Structures and Algorithms), this is the holy grail. It provides video explanations for the top 150 LeetCode questions you need to know.

## 🤝 Get Involved: GSU CS Clubs

Networking is just as important as studying. Joining a club is the easiest way to make friends in your major, build projects, and get referrals. 

*   **Computer Science Club (CSC)**: The main hub for CS students. Great for general networking, guest speaker events, and finding study buddies.
*   **ProgSU**: Focused on competitive programming and algorithmic problem solving. Great if you want to sharpen your coding skills!
*   **PantherDev**: The perfect club if you want to build real-world software projects, learn about web/app development, and build your portfolio.
*   **Cybersecurity Club**: Interested in hacking, network security, or defense? This club hosts workshops, Capture The Flag (CTF) events, and industry talks.

*(Tip: Search for these clubs on the Panther Involvement Network (PIN) or ask around for their Discord links!)*

## 🧠 General College Survival Tips (For Any Major)

### 📅 Mastering Your Schedule
*   **Stack Your Classes**: If you commute to downtown Atlanta, try to stack your classes onto 2 or 3 days a week. Saving yourself the commute time (and MARTA/parking money) is a massive stress reliever.
*   **Automate Your Calendar**: Use **[Reclaim AI](https://reclaim.ai/)** to manage your schedule. It automatically blocks out time for studying, commuting, and habits based on your Google Calendar.
*   **Overriding Credit Hours**: Did you know you can get an override to take more than the standard maximum credit hours? Talk to your advisor if you are trying to graduate early or tackle a double major.
*   **The "Full Class" Hack**: If a class you desperately need is full, **email the professor directly**. Professors can request an override for up to 2 extra students to join a full section!

### 📚 Acing Your Classes
*   **Make Group Chats**: On the first day of every class, create a GroupMe, Discord, or WhatsApp chat and write the link on the whiteboard (or drop it in the Zoom/Webex chat). Surviving CS is a team sport.
*   **Go to Office Hours**: Seriously, go. Even if you just sit there and do your homework, building a relationship with your professor is invaluable for letters of recommendation and networking.
*   **Use Tutoring Services**: Don't wait until you are failing to get help. Utilize GSU's tutoring centers and platforms like **TutorOcean** to get past roadblocks in your coding assignments.
